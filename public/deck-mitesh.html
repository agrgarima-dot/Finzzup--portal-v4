How It Works

From onboarding to
ongoing financial clarity

1
Discovery call & scoping
30-minute call to understand your business model, current financial state, key pain points, and what decisions you need better data to make. Garima proposes the right pack and scope.
2
Onboarding & data setup
Garima maps your chart of accounts, connects to your accounting software (Tally, Zoho Books, QuickBooks), and sets up MIS templates calibrated to your business. Portal access is provisioned.
3
Monthly operating rhythm
By the 7th of each month: you upload the previous month's data. By the 15th: your CFO Brief, P&L, and cashflow report are ready on the portal. Monthly review call to walk through findings and actions.
4
Strategic support on demand
Board decks, investor data rooms, banker negotiations, compliance notices, new entity structuring — Garima is available throughout the month via WhatsApp and email, not just on scheduled calls.
5
Quarterly business review
Every quarter, a deeper session covering trailing 3-month performance, forward plan vs. actuals, compliance health check, and a refresh of the 12-month financial model.
